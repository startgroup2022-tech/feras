## Remaining gaps (the PARTIAL rows above)

Each item below is a screen that exists and is reachable but does not yet reach the deck's interface. None of these is a business rule or a permission change -- they are missing fields or missing controls.


## How to read this matrix

- **OK** -- every control the deck names for the slide is present in the running SPA or answers on its endpoint.
- **PARTIAL** -- the screen exists and is reachable, but a named element is missing. The gap is listed in the Remaining gaps section; nothing is hidden behind an auto-OK.
- **REF** -- a spec, dictionary, registry or acceptance slide that maps to no single screen. These are reviewed by hand, not scored by signal.
- A mapping with no `signals` is never scored OK by default: a group must carry evidence, or it is left as REF.
- **Visual** -- the slide's *rendered* frame vs the mapped screen's rendered pixels (`deck_visual_parity.py`). `OK` means the app paints the deck's frame (light surface, RTL rail where the deck draws one, a non-empty content band, and a multi-tile KPI strip on the role slides); `-` means the slide maps to no screen; `?` means it maps to a screen whose screenshot was not available to probe; `FAIL` would list the slide in the parity line above. This column is deliberately independent of the text evidence: a slide can pass the text checks and still fail the visual one.
